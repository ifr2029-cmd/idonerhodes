Film

As an associate producer at SALTY Features, I assist with the independent distribution of several films in our slate. I conduct outreach and develop relationships with exhibitors, primarily focusing on community and education organizations.

I am interested in curating from archives of moving image materials for public exhibition. I have interned at Anthology Film Archives and have worked in various production and curatorial capacities for the San Francisco International Film Festival, the Montclair Film Festival, the Woods Hole Film Festival, and the Yale Student Film Festival.

I worked as an intern at the West Newton Cinema in Newton, Massachuetts as the theater was transitioning into a nonprofit. I then worked as the Cinema's Programming and Outreach Coordinator, helping them develop their special film events and screening series, including a four-day festival featuring local filmmakers.
