Idone Rhodes

I am currently a graduate student at the NYU Tisch School of the Arts, where I am studying for an MA in Moving Image Archiving and Preservation. I also work as an associate producer at SALTY Features, an independent film production company based in New York City.

I graduated from Yale University with a BA in English and Film and Media Studies and was a Yale Journalism Scholar. I have experience working in both production and programming at film festivals and independent movie theaters.
